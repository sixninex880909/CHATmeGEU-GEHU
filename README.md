GEU STUDENT CONNECT
A Platform for Students to Connect, Ask & Help
Project Overview

GEU Student Connect is our PBL project, made specifically for the students of Graphic Era University.

The basic idea is simple — sometimes students get stuck with assignments, subjects, projects, exams, internships, or other college-related problems and don't really know whom to ask. We want to create one platform where they can simply post their problem and get help from seniors, faculty members, or other students.

The concept is a little similar to Reddit, where people can create posts and discuss different topics. The main difference is that our platform will be focused only on the GEU student community, so the discussions and information will be more relevant to students here.

Objectives
Make it easier for students to ask for help.
Connect juniors with seniors and faculty members.
Give students one place to discuss college-related problems.
Allow students to find answers to questions that have already been asked.
Organize discussions into topics like academics, projects, exams, internships, placements, etc.
Use what we are learning in Data Structures and OOP in a real project.
Work together as a team using Git and GitHub.

Future Work

We are planning to add features like student profiles, creating posts, comments, replies, categories, search, upvotes, notifications, and reporting.

Later, we can also add things like college email verification, anonymous questions, direct messaging, placement and internship sections, and a mobile app.

The main goal is to make GEU Student Connect something that students can actually use when they need help or want to share their experience with others.
